Which rule is working: the scoreboard

Every past session in the Pick Tracker log is replayed. For each rule, take the stocks it would have picked that day and compare their move 2 and 4 weeks later (10 and 20 sessions) with the average of every tracked Bursa stock (the "market"). The page then recommends the rule that is leading: if the Top 30 leads, the plan is a plain rotation into the Top 30; if another rule leads, the plan switches to it; if nothing beats the market, it says so. This re-runs every day as the log grows.

Your portfolio vs the plan

Where your money is

Rules: TRIM = bigger than the max-per-stock setting (rounded up to whole board lots of 100). REVIEW EXIT = closed below the 3 × ATR risk line from your average cost, a SELL signal, a KDJ Exit, or a downtrend. KEEP = picked today by the recommended rule or the runner-up. ★ COMBO = 12-month winner still in an uptrend near its high (the lasting strategy in section 1). DOWNTREND = below its 200-day average with the 50-day under the 200-day; it counts as a REVIEW EXIT reason. GREEN = closed below its weekly average (Entry EMA20, 5 sessions), the one box pattern that held in your 5-year Bursa backtest. Holdings come from KLSE_Trade_Journal.xlsx via the dashboard.

3

Buy list

Sizing splits the budget equally across the BUY rows from the recommended rule (most 6), capped at the max-per-stock setting, in two entries: the latest close and Support (lower BB), or one ATR lower if it is already under Support. Shares are rounded down to board lots of 100; if half the allocation is under one lot, it all goes in at Support. Runner-up rows are shown for reference and are not funded. Stocks with a SELL signal are not funded. Results inside the next 10 days are flagged: a gap can skip straight past your entry. Ringgit figures exclude brokerage, stamp duty and clearing fees.

Closed trades & dividends

Every SELL and DIVIDEND row in KLSE_Trade_Journal.xlsx from the start month, grouped by month (sale date / payment date), newest month first. Tap a month to expand or collapse it; tap a column heading to sort within each month. Realized P&L = sale value − fees − average cost × shares sold, the same average-cost method as the journal. Opened = the day the position last went from zero to held; "carried over" means it came in as an OPENING balance on 14 Oct 2026, so its true buy date isn't in the journal. Partial = some shares are still held. DIVIDEND rows: Price = dividend per share, Amount = shares × DPS − fees, % = yield on your average cost; they don't count as wins.

Built from dashboard-data.json (section 6: your trade journal), dip_watch_data.json (section 5), pick_tracker_data.json and valuation_data.json, so it refreshes with the daily 7 PM pipeline run. Rules come from the Pick Tracker, your Bursa backtest (backtest_box_momentum.py, 2026-10-12) and the replay in section 1. Results dates marked "due by" are Bursa listing-rule deadlines (2 months after quarter end), not announced dates. Past patterns over a few months of data do not guarantee future results.
